# Validation boundaries

This delivery concerns executable engineering and routing conditional on declared research constructions. Uljin is a development integration case. It is not an independent validation incident.

The preserved original hourly forecast is a marginal endpoint forecast. Its retrospective arrival labels, local-contact prediction population, input support and known outputs remain identifiable. The constructor adds a target-to-flame mapping, an occurrence/dependence/timing law, initial active-fire state, missing-source assignments and illustrative physical parameters. Those additions are assumptions. They do not recover observations or validate the original model.

Strict mode reports missing physical information. Research mode supplies a finite ensemble of assumed futures on the original grid. Complete constructed support means the declared finite-domain construction can be evaluated; it does not mean the real fire is fully observed. Cells outside the prediction population remain unknown in the original forecast. Exterior-domain sources remain excluded.

Any-contact interval flame masks and nonnegative source-union radiation bounds enclose the declared source histories during each representation interval. They can reject an underlying scenario route that would have been feasible with exact event timing, and the router's conditional optimum refers to the supplied interval enclosure, lattice, graph and ensemble. Representation intervals do not create subhour predictive accuracy.

The temporal bound applies at native cell-center receivers. Extending each value throughout its 375 m routing cell is a spatial surrogate, not a proven maximum over continuous road positions. The float64 FFT has an explicit peak-scaled numerical guard and independent direct-source checks; these establish bounded numerical agreement, not a formal interval-arithmetic certificate.

Radiation excludes convection, smoke, embers, terrain shielding, uncertain road passability and omitted exterior sources. Combustion and receiver geometry are illustrative. The retained peak and dose budgets are study inputs from the prior routing control; they are not clinical or firefighter limits. No physical-safety claim is made.

Routing coverage must include travel, waiting and destination dwell. The one-hour horizon is not repeated. Later historical cutoffs are later information. A feed gap cannot be crossed without supported exposure history. Conservative history transfer pairs any new supplied future with any old supplied history and carries an upper bound on prior dose; it does not assert that independently reconstructed futures are persistent physical realizations.

Primary TIMEOUT stays unresolved. A checked feasible witness certifies that witness under the supplied contract. It does not certify global optimality unless exact search completed, and absence of a witness does not establish infeasibility. Existing recorded-return checker limitations are retained.

Unit, source-preservation, numerical, tiny-reference, regression and fresh-extraction checks establish only their documented bounded software properties. They do not establish calibration, forecast-value advantage, field efficacy, usability, deployment suitability, ensemble coverage or real-fire thermal validation. Missing same-fire thermal and active-flame observations remain unavailable.
