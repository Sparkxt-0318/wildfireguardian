# Work accounting scope

Fresh source construction and centre admission contribute **30.965016 measured wall seconds** and **32.294404 measured CPU seconds**, once for three sources, in addition to the search/check panel. The source receipt starts after imports. Interpreter startup/imports, initial graph loading and final cleanup for that preparation process were not independently captured: their complete CPU/wall cost is **unavailable**, not zero. This is a disclosed accounting limitation; no total-project or general efficiency gain claim is made.

Every development and fresh search mode/case worker is externally timed through interpreter exit, including imports, input loading, source re-admission, immutable graph preparation/cache ownership, cold/warm searches, final binding, road checks and failed work. Sum complete launch cost once; do not add inclusive component/cold/warm timers again. The original solver 30-second limit excludes that separately reported external epoch preparation, while including solver admission, heuristic work, search, centre checking and finalization. The whole-road checker has a distinct 30-second limit. The 180-second process watchdog does not enlarge either solver/checker cap.

RSS is lifetime process peak, and warm RSS includes the cold phase. Template shallow storage is a partial memory description and excludes nested Fraction/Python storage. Failures with unavailable phase costs remain unavailable. Portable watchdog termination retains measured wall and reaped-child CPU; complete descendant CPU/RSS can be unavailable. Portable tests and independent final review have their own process receipts and are not attributed to routing latency.

The retained source-binding failure, pre-guard corrected development, original-cap observer reproductions, constructor preparation, author/reference/provenance controls and portable verification remain separate evidence. Development full-process work is in `evidence/DEVELOPMENT_FULL_PROCESS_WORK.json`; source preparation stages are in `evidence/FRESH_PREPARATION_WORK_SCOPE.json`. Work from earlier releases is preserved rather than recharged as a new experiment.
