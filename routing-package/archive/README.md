manifest and review/measurement records from the 5 October integration delivery.

byte-for-byte. These records were copied; no study was rerun or refrozen.

Archived scripts are **not executable entry points for this reorganized layout**.
Their relative paths and absolute research-workspace dependencies intentionally
remain as originally delivered. Run historical experiments in the original
delivery, following its original reproduction guide and measurement coordination.

The candidate's historical BENCHMARKS, CLAIMS_AND_LIMITS, OWNER_HANDOFF and
REPRODUCE documents are retained in `delivery/docs/`. The candidate's historical
INPUT_READINESS record is retained in `delivery/`. The authoritative instructions for this
organized release are the root README/REPRODUCE and `docs/REFACTOR_REPORT.md`.

`FILE_MANIFEST.json` and `verification/REFRACTOR_FREEZE.json` do that.
