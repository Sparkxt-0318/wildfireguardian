**3.4 Egress policy — `same_route` vs `free`.**

- At `t_load` ≤ 5: **identical, 62 = 62.** ⚠ This is the important half of
  the answer, and it constrains how the claim may be stated: **the egress
  *timing* (evaluating the return leg at the egress departure time) is doing
  the work; the egress *policy* is not.** The finding does not depend on the
  N = 1 same-route doctrine.
- At `t_load` ≥ 10: `free` is **stricter** (81), not laxer. The mechanism is
  visible in the finite-margin counts (142 → 126 → 104 → 96 → 79 as `t_load`
  goes 0 → 20): under `free`, more homes get **no safe egress route at all**
  at the egress departure time (margin = −∞) rather than a small negative
  margin. ⚠ Consequence, stated so nobody misreads the artifact: the `free`
  arm's *median finite margin rises* with `t_load` (28.1 → 69.7) purely by
  survivorship — the negative cases leave the finite set. That median is not
  a safety improvement and must never be quoted as one.
