## Thesis (two sentences)

⚠⚠ **Corrected 2026-09-07T1100Z by critic #34; the previous sentence is quoted in the note below because it is the root objection.** A route planner and a rescue order that read a hazard field beat ones that ignore hazard entirely, and the repository proves *that* on committed public data for real Korean fires with every number re-derived by a gate. **What it has not measured is the step from 「where the fire is」 to 「where it will be」**: both headline margins are scored against a fire-blind opponent, and the forecast-aware arm plans on the same field it is scored against, so 42 and 91 are what a **noiseless** forecast would buy.
