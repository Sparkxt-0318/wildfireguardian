# Expert replies, 2026-09-04 — what three people wrote back, paraphrased

*The author wrote to a number of experts on 2026-09-04 (NH-010, NH-023) and three replied the same day. By the author's decision (Claude Code session, 2026-09-05) they are named here with a close paraphrase and no verbatim quotation; the originals stay in the author's private notes. Nothing below goes beyond what each person actually said.*

## 이해평 (강원대학교 소방방재학부)

Near retirement and no longer active in research, so he declined detailed technical advice and said explicitly that he does not know the project well enough to comment further. His own past interest was predicting fire-spread behaviour from terrain, vegetation and weather. He raised two questions worth answering:

1. Whether prioritising rescue need **by age alone** is appropriate as the population ages (→ WFG-090).
2. Whether the results can be used in practice without considering **how forest-fire suppression and residential emergency response divide their roles** during a fire (→ WFG-091).

## Volker Radeloff (University of Wisconsin–Madison, SILVIS Lab)

Cannot say whether the US wildland–urban interface (WUI) concept transfers to rural Korea, but was surprised how well it held in other countries he tested. Two concrete pointers:

- Look at SILVIS's global WUI map for familiar parts of Korea and judge whether it looks right: https://geoserver.silvis.forest.wisc.edu/geodata/fast/globalwui/
- If it does not, the distance thresholds behind the global map may be the reason; the method is Carlson et al. (2022), *Ecological Applications*, "The wildland-urban interface in the United States based on 125 million buildings" (→ WFG-092; see also `docs/auto/knowledge/WUI_BUILDINGS_AS_FUEL.md`).

## John P. Wilson (University of Southern California, Spatial Sciences Institute)

One specific point: pedestrians can leave mapped paths in a way vehicles cannot, so capacity-aware shortest-path routing designed for road-bound vehicles may not transfer cleanly to evacuation on foot (→ WFG-093; see `docs/auto/knowledge/ROUTING_FUNDAMENTALS.md`).
