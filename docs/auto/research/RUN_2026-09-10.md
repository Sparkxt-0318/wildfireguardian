## 2. What is new, and what it does to this project's claims

### 2.1 Somebody built this project's pipeline and published it on 2026-09-09

**This is the run's headline, and it is good news stated carefully.** Bokade, Rahul; Barai, Vishal; Bhogle, Shravani; Chapherkar, Yug; Kale, Dilip (supervisor), 2026-09-09, 「A Hybrid Neural Physical Framework for Wildfire Propagation Modeling and Dynamic Evacuation Routing」, preprint on Zenodo, <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22668357> (duplicate record 22668358) [record and abstract opened].

**What they did.** A U-Net supplies a per-cell flammability prior; an explicit **cellular-automaton physics engine inside PyTorch** performs the temporal spread with wind advection and convective heating; a **dual-layer router** — OpenRouteService vector routing with spatial polygon clustering, plus an internal **D\* Lite** incremental-replanning grid fallback — 「incrementally routes traffic away from actively predicted fires」. Study area **Uttarakhand, India**. **Metric: none.** The record reports no evaluation of the routing layer and no numerical result of any kind; the claim is architectural.

**How it changes this project's claims — three ways, and the third is the one that matters.**

1. **The architecture is not the contribution, and can no longer be presented as one.** Routing against a *forecast* hazard field rather than a current perimeter has been this project's implicit design novelty. An independent group made the same choice in the same month. This does not weaken the project; it relocates its claim.
2. **The relocation lands somewhere stronger and already built.** They ship 「an interactive simulation environment」 and evaluate nothing. This project ships a per-point walk-or-be-rescued verdict **and** `docs/disc_null.md`, `docs/oracle_gap.md` and a withdrawn-claims registry measuring how wrong that verdict can be. The differentiator is the **output object and its measured limits**, and it is now demonstrable against a same-month, same-architecture comparator instead of asserted. Filed as **WFG-239**.
3. **⚠ Their headline negative result is this project's own uncomfortable finding, independently reproduced.** Verbatim from the abstract: 「modern predictive frameworks utilizing end-to-end deep learning often succumb to **persistence bias**, wherein models achieve high statistical accuracy by propagating current fire states forward while ignoring fluid dynamics」, and 「we ... show empirically that **neither a recurrent nor a non-recurrent network learns to advect fire past the persistence baseline**」. That is why they abandoned learned advection for an explicit CA engine.

   **WFG-234 already asks this project to score a persistence null against its own headline truth, and already warns that persistence 「may well beat the model at short horizons」.** `docs/disc_null.md` §4 has already measured the neighbouring fact — the model's core centre of mass travels 3,646.1 m where the observed footprint's travels 1,124.8 m, and the stationary disc ends up closer to the truth than the model. **If persistence wins when WFG-234 runs, that is not a private embarrassment: it is a named, published failure mode of the entire model class, measured independently in the same month, and this project would be among the few to have measured it on its own headline number.** WFG-234's row was updated in place with this framing and the instruction to pre-register it in the claim commit.

⚠ **Boundaries, load-bearing:** Uttarakhand; a road/vehicle routing stack; **no reported metric**; an **unrefereed Zenodo preprint** whose attached file is an IEEE conference template, which is not an IEEE publication. It licenses a **framing** and supplies **no number**. Nothing from it is registered. ⚠ And one sentence in it is the overclaim this project must not imitate: its abstract closes on 「guaranteed safe evacuation」.

### 2.4 Pyrogeography: two Korean sources and one lattice model

- **TFFM** (Florez, Suárez Morell, Millan & Restrepo, arXiv:2609.07763, 2026-09-07 [opened]): terrain-weighted lattice fire model, β_c **decreasing with terrain roughness**, downhill asymmetry suppressing spread, front-velocity exponent **δ = 0.34 ± 0.02**, wind raising β_c by **2–4×**, lattices to L = 8192. Independent mechanism-level support for slope and wind as first-order directional controls. ⚠ An abstract lattice model; its exponents are critical-phenomena quantities comparable with nothing here. Licenses no feature change and no refit.
- **Spring green-up gating** (Choi & Chae, Zenodo 22253316, 2026-09-02 [opened]): large wildfires aligned on a **days-since-local-green-up** axis across **South Korea**, the Mongolian Plateau and the Northern Hemisphere (GlobFire 2002–2021, MCD12Q2 green-up, permutation tests, ERA5-Land weather adjustment). The sharper version of §2's Korean spring-season account: the season may be gated by **phenology** rather than by calendar. ⚠ What was opened is the **reproducibility archive, not the journal article**; the finding statement is the archive's own, with no metric read.
- **Which dryness index predicts Korean ignition** (Min, Sunghyun, Zenodo 22115424, v3 2026-09-06 [opened]): case-crossover over **5,783 ignitions and 22,936 matched controls, South Korea 1997–2013**, comparing effective humidity (실효습도), an equilibrium-moisture index, ten-hour fuel moisture and hourly FFMC. ⚠ **Numbers embargoed to 2027-05-30**; the comparative claim is **the title's** and no magnitude may be cited.
