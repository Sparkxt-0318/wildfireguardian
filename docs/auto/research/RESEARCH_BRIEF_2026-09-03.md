## (d) The real scientific contribution to pursue

**What survives from R3 after two independent refutations:** the project is not, and cannot become, a state-of-the-art spread model (n = 6 vs 607 fires in WildfireSpreadTS, https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.12003 ; AUC and the field's AP are not comparable and the model card already says so). Its distinctness is (i) the **coupling** — an event-held-out, calibrated P(ignite) field consumed by a time-expanded, exposure-minimising pedestrian router at sampled-origin resolution, with a rescue-ingress survival term and operator outputs, evaluated by paired contrasts on real OSM networks; no vehicle-centric analogue (RESCUE ICDCN 2026 https://doi.org/10.1145/3772290.3772301 ; Borgwardt et al. https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.14500) does the rescue/household side — and (ii) the **evaluation-design discipline** (LOFO + far-band + a 260-entry registry gated by `make verify`, withdrawn claims kept in the tree), which the field is now publishing as a problem (WSTS+ covariate shift; "does your model work or just score well?" https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.18911).

**What was refuted and must not be started:**
- C1 "value-of-forecast regime experiment": the key cell (trained hazard on the 124 buildings) was measured in Session 15 (24 vs 25 failing, ρ = 0.99, in-sample) and every control (null, no-DEM, ignition prior/count, clearance diagnostic) already exists; the "contradiction" it resolves is a merge of incommensurable layers that §5.23/24 forbid; with 24 failing units at one site it has no power.
