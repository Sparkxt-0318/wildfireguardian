## 2. Where WildfireGuardian actually sits (blunt)

1. **As a spread model it is not state of the art and cannot be made so in 6 weeks or 6 months.** n = 6 fires against 607 (WSTS) or 5,435 (CFSDS). Its per-overpass label is arguably *more* operational than next-day masks, but that is a framing point, not a result. Any attempt to sell the AUC will be beaten by one question about prevalence. The card already knows this.
2. **Its genuine distinctness is the coupling**: a calibrated, event-held-out `P(ignite)` field → time-expanded, exposure-minimising *pedestrian* routing at *household* resolution → rescue-ingress survival → operator/delivery outputs, on real OSM networks, with paired counterfactual contrasts. Nothing in #21–#25 does all of that; RESCUE and the Portuguese ABM are vehicle-centric, the trigger-buffer line is community-level and simulator-driven, the shelter MIPs are static-risk.
3. **Its second distinctness is procedural**: LOFO + far-band + a 260-entry number registry gated by `make verify`, with withdrawn claims kept in the tree. Papers #3 and #9 show the field is now publishing *exactly* the failure modes WG documented (cross-event shift, metric-dependent conclusions). That is a methods contribution if it is written as one.
4. **Its biggest scientific embarrassment is internal, not external**: the README leads with "42/458 origins are safe *only* because the router knows where the fire will be", while Session 17 shows the fire changes *nothing* about which households fail at 240 min on the elliptical hazard. These are different layers (road-scan origins on the trained field vs households on the elliptical field), but a judge will hear "fire matters / fire doesn't matter". Until this is reconciled with one controlled experiment, the project cannot state cleanly what the forecast is *for*.
5. **Its biggest external embarrassment is #18**: 84.5 % of survivors drove, 3.1 % walked. The resident-side product is a walking route.

### Must-answer objection (from #18) and the honest answer
*"Why route pedestrians when 85 % drove and only 9 of 291 walked?"* — The honest answer is that the walking layer is not a mass-evacuation product; it is a **classifier for who cannot self-evacuate** (no own car: ~40 %; living alone in Yeongdeok: 36 %; the 8 Yeongdeok dead are not in the survey). Its output is the input to the rescue/dispatch layer and to the 이장 A4 sheet, which the same survey shows was the channel that worked. The project should say this in one sentence and never again imply that villages walk out. It also should register the survey figures (car 84.5 %, foot 3.1 %, no-text 38 %, Yeongdeok text receipt 48 %) as L0 evidence entries and use them to replace the unexplained 0.30 immobility assumption with a survey-bracketed range (see C3).

## 3. Candidate real contributions

Effort scale: hours / days / weeks. "Killer objection" = the one thing that would make a competent reviewer discard it.

### C1 — "When does the forecast change who fails?" — the value-of-forecast regime experiment  *(6 weeks; KCF first)*
- **Claim.** The learned spread forecast changes evacuation outcomes only for origins whose free-flow walking margin to the nearest surviving refuge lies inside a band set by the hazard's reach over the horizon; outside that band the outcome is geometric. State the band and show the 42/458 (trained field) and 0/24 (elliptical field) results as two points on one curve.
- **Why new.** #3, #6, #7, #9 all argue that accuracy metrics do not measure operational value; none quantify *decision* value for evacuation. Trigger-buffer papers (#24) assume the fire model and ask about evacuation time; nobody holds the network fixed and varies forecast informativeness to find where routing decisions flip. Internally it resolves item 4 above.
- **Experiment (all public data, all code exists).** Re-run `scripts/vulnerability_tautology.py` with `TrainedModelHazard` (canonical field) instead of the elliptical hazard, at Yeongdeok and the two other regions; sweep (i) horizon 60–360 min (already in Session 15), (ii) hazard informativeness: null → elliptical → trained → trained with the arrival-time survival buffer (BLOCKERS future-work item 2), (iii) ignition prior inside vs outside the household cluster. Report, per cell of the grid, the size of the non-tautological failing set (b) and the "FA-only" origin share. Add the two BLOCKERS diagnostics (does `clearance_margin_min` ever become finite; why proxy and control disagree on 6 households). Preregister the grid in a one-page doc before running.
- **Success metric.** A monotone relationship between hazard reach (e.g. 90th-percentile time-to-cutoff distance from households) and |(b)|; a stated crossover; the 42/458 and 0/24 numbers reproduced as grid cells with no committed value changed. Negative result (fire never bites anywhere) is also reportable and is *stronger* for the refuge-placement story.
- **Effort.** Days (2–4 sessions). Killer objection: *"you tuned the hazard until it mattered."* Defence: preregistered grid, every cell reported, the null and elliptical cells are the committed ones.

### C2 — Risk-controlled operating threshold under leave-one-fire-out conformal risk control  *(6 weeks; KCF second / IEEE core)*
- **Claim.** Replace the untuned `forward_sim_advance_threshold = 0.3` with a threshold λ chosen so that the fraction of actually-ignited cells missed on a *held-out fire* is ≤ α (say 0.2), with a finite-sample bound under exchangeability of fires; report how the routing partition (414/42/2 and the three-region table) moves, and what fraction of cells is flagged.
- **Why new.** #8 (Dayan 2026) is the only prior CRC work in wildfire and stops at pixel maps on one US dataset. No one has (i) done it event-held-out, (ii) carried the guaranteed field into a time-expanded router, (iii) reported the routing consequence. It converts the card's weakest sentence ("a DEFAULT, not a tuned value") into a principled choice with a stated guarantee — the exact question a judge asks.
- **Experiment.** Out-of-fold predictions already exist (`spread_v2_lofo_oof_cells.csv.gz`, positional join verified). Nested protocol: for each test fire *f*, calibrate λ_f on the OOF predictions of the other five fires (OOF ⇒ no leakage), evaluate empirical FNR and flagged fraction on *f*; then run the routing integration with λ = median λ_f (or per-region λ) as a new arm. Also report the per-fire spread of FNR — that spread *is* the exchangeability caveat made visible. Implementation is ~150 lines on top of `oof_metrics.py`; the CRC bound is Angelopoulos et al. 2024 (https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/file/f3549ef9b5ff520a7e41ff3cc306ab2b-Paper-Conference.pdf).
- **Success metric.** Empirical held-out FNR ≤ α on ≥ 5/6 fires at the calibrated λ; flagged-pixel fraction; routing partition under λ vs 0.3, with the number of origins that change bucket. Also report what α the current 0.3 corresponds to (probably FNR ≈ 0.86 pooled — recall 0.138 — which is itself a headline).
- **Effort.** Days. Killer objection: *"six fires are not exchangeable and cells within a fire are dependent, so the bound is decorative."* Defence: the guarantee is stated as conditional on fire-exchangeability, the per-fire FNR range is the honest disclosure, and the same objection applies to every LOFO number the project already reports. Do not claim the bound is tight.

### C3 — Survey-bracketed mobility parameters and walking-speed sensitivity  *(6 weeks; add-on, hours–days)*
- **Claim.** The assumed immobility rate 0.30 and walking speed 0.7 m/s are bracketed by public evidence: 40 % of 2025 survivors had no own car, 3.1 % walked, 36 % of Yeongdeok respondents live alone (#18); community-dwelling 65+ gait 0.92 ± 0.27 m/s (Studenski 2011). Sweep speed 0.5–1.2 m/s and immobility 0.2–0.5 and report how the 458 partition and the 24-household failing set move.
- **Why new.** Trivially not new as method; new as *grounding* — no Korean evacuation model has been parameterised from a post-fire survivor survey. It removes the "where does 0.30 come from" question.
- **Experiment.** Add speed to the existing `--sweep fc` machinery; register the survey figures as L0 entries with the PDF sha256.
- **Success metric.** A 2-D table; identify whether any committed conclusion flips inside the bracket. Effort: hours–1 day. Killer objection: survivor bias (the dead did not answer). Say so.

### C4 — Sub-daily GK2A labels and directional sensitivity (preregistered plan, plan-B arm)  *(6 months; IEEE)*
- **Claim.** H_res as already preregistered (gk2a_direction_experiment §1, §5).
- **What changed in the literature.** #13/#14 show Korean groups now produce GK2A-derived detection (12.9 min lag) and 2-min extent tracking (F1 0.73–0.96) over the very fires WG uses. Their detectors are not public (**UNVERIFIED**), so plan-B still needs an in-house BTD detector — but its validation target is now concrete (match VNP14IMG at F1 ≥ ~0.7 on Uiseong 2025 before any label is used).
- **Effort.** Weeks (detector + validation + relabel + LOFO on the 4-fire subset). Killer objection: 2 km pixel vs 500 m cell; already listed as confounder 1. Second objection: ERA5 wind is unchanged, so a null may be an instrument null (confounder 4) — this is why LDAPS (§14.6) belongs in the same paper.

### C5 — Fire-aware refuge placement across three regions  *(6 months; IEEE or Korean journal)*
- **Claim.** Placement that maximises reachability-under-hazard *and* refuge survival on the trained field differs from reachability-only placement (Session 22) and from static-risk MIP placement (#25) in a stated number of households, at three sites.
- **Why new.** #25 (Uiryeong) and Nunes 2024 use static risk and aggregate units; Session 22 is reachability-only with 0/120 survival events. Nobody has placed rural pedestrian refuges against a LOFO-validated dynamic hazard.
- **Experiment.** Extend `refuge_placement.py` objective with the survival filter and trained field; run at Yeongdeok, Uiseong-Andong, Uljin-Samcheok; Jaccard of saved-household sets across objectives and horizons.
- **Effort.** Weeks. Killer objection: OSM building sparsity (74–124 buildings at Yeongdeok vs 988–1,763 abroad) makes every household count a proxy; the real footprints (도로명주소) are login-gated (BLOCKERS S8). Alternative national footprint sources for Korea: **UNVERIFIED** (Google Open Buildings does not cover Korea; Microsoft Global ML Footprints coverage of Korea not checked).

### C6 — Evaluation design for small-N event datasets: a documented case  *(6 months; IEEE methods paper, pairs with C2)*
- **Claim.** On a six-event dataset, (i) pooled, mean-of-folds and far-band AUC give opposite answers to the same ablation; (ii) the top permutation-importance feature is an acquisition artifact that hurts transfer; (iii) a DEM defect in one fire moved far-band AUC by −0.036 across *every* fold; (iv) platform drift bounds reportable precision to three figures; (v) US zero-shot transfer collapses (max p 0.013–0.027) while pipeline portability holds. All already measured; the contribution is to assemble them as a reproducible cautionary protocol with the registry as the instrument.
- **Why new.** #9 and #3 make the same argument on large US benchmarks with foundation models; nobody has shown it on a small-N national dataset where LOFO is the *only* honest protocol, and nobody has shipped a claim-registry gate as the fix. Meyer & Pebesma 2022 / Ploton 2020 are the spatial-CV foundations; WG is the event-CV instance.
- **Experiment.** Mostly writing; one new arm — re-run LOFO with the A4 configuration (drop `days_since_rain`) and with 24-h precipitation as *new registry entries*, never replacing committed ones. Optionally merge the `us-acquisition` Arm-0 record.
- **Effort.** Weeks. Killer objection: *"this is an audit of your own bugs, not science."* Counter: the field is now publishing exactly this (#3, #9), and the registry is a transferable tool.

### C7 — Rescue dispatch as a stochastic program vs the heuristic ordering  *(6 months; lowest priority)*
- **Claim.** Formulate WG's rescue side as the supported-evacuation problem of #2608.05413 and compare against the committed ordering.
- **Why not first.** The consultant says the operational window `W` does not exist in the field, and dispatch_ordering already shows deadline sorting wins 0/180 configurations. Building an optimiser on a non-existent parameter is the wrong axis. Only worth doing if the objective is reformulated around ingress-survival alone. Effort: weeks–months. Killer objection: no capacity data, no field `W`.

### (f) Generalisation of the LOFO + evidence-registry discipline
The pattern — event-grouped held-out validation, a machine-checked registry of every reportable number, retired-number scanning, arm isolation, and withdrawn claims kept in the tree — transfers unchanged to any environmental-mapping task where events are few and spatially clustered: flood-extent mapping from SAR (per-flood LOEO), landslide susceptibility (per-storm), harmful-algal-bloom detection (per-bloom), wildfire-smoke PM2.5 (per-fire; cf. https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.07951). The nearest existing tooling is showyourwork (CI-rebuilt papers) and NNDM-LOO CV (Milà 2022); neither gates *claims*. A short tools paper (JOSS or IEEE Software) is plausible but is not a KCF deliverable.
