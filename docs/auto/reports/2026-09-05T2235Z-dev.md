### 1. What was built

have asked for. The committed headline (91 of 368 origins safe only on the forecast-aware

The new arm is the planner a county emergency office actually has: the fire perimeter **as it
is now**, dilated by a fixed safety margin, and no knowledge of how it will move. It is the
identical router (`future_aware_route`) run against a frozen `HazardSequence` — every slice

path is then graded against the **true** hazard by `_evaluate_path`, the committed scorer.
Graph, refuges, budget, `p_cut`, time step and origin rule are the committed ones.

also **re-derives the committed 91 node-for-node** before reporting anything new, and the
registrar exits 2 rather than register if that flag is false.
