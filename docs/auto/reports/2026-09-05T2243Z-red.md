### 1. What was built

`scripts/run_present_perimeter_arm.py` adds the **fair opponent** the last four critic laps
have asked for. The committed headline (91 of 368 origins safe only on the forecast-aware
route) beats a baseline that is **fire-blind**; beating it shows that looking at the fire is

The new arm is the planner a county emergency office actually has: the fire perimeter **as it
is now**, dilated by a fixed safety margin, and no knowledge of how it will move. It is the
identical router (`future_aware_route`) run against a frozen `HazardSequence` — every slice

path is then graded against the **true** hazard by `_evaluate_path`, the committed scorer.
Graph, refuges, budget, `p_cut`, time step and origin rule are the committed ones.

also **re-derives the committed 91 node-for-node** before reporting anything new, and the
registrar exits 2 rather than register if that flag is false.

### 2. The number, whatever it says

Same 368 origins, same refuges, same budget — **how many reach a refuge safely:**

| planner | safe |
|---|---:|
| fire-blind baseline (what the committed headline beats) | **265** |

Of the committed **91** forecast-aware-only origins, the 1 km opponent also saves **79**.
Twelve remain forecast-only, **none** of them burned by the opponent (zero unsafe routes at
1 km): **8 are cut off from every refuge by the safety margin itself**, and **4 are inside
the margin and told not to move.**

And the 8 are mostly not a win for the forecast either. Of the 25 origins the 1 km arm walls
off, 11 have a forecast-aware route, and **10 of those 11 escape across ground that never
burns at all** — 80 of the 203 refused cells never catch fire. Only 1 escapes across ground
that does burn later, the only case where knowing the *timing* did any work. **The honest
reading of the residual gap is "the 1 km buffer was too wide", not "the forecast was
clever."** Both gaps (27, and 5 at the best buffer) are also **upper** bounds: this opponent
never re-plans, and the forecast arm is graded on the field it was shown.

**Why it nearly ties, which is the real finding:** the slice-0 perimeter dilated by 1 km
already contains **93.9 %** of the cells burning at the 720-minute horizon. On this fire the
envelope grows by less than the margin, so a static buffer is a near-substitute for the
forecast. Whether that fails on a faster fire is a **prediction this repository has not
tested**, and the doc says so rather than implying it.
