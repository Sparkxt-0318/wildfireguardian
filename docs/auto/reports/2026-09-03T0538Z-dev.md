### The root objection I recorded before building (`hate`)

*"Making the sandbox green by adding skips is how a suite becomes a suite that
proves nothing. Five failures become five skips, the summary line says green,
and coverage went down."* The cheapest test is per-failure: could this assertion
pass in a clean clone if the code were right? Of the twelve red items, seven
genuinely could not (the DEM raster is never in a clone) and are honest skips;
two were a guard checking the wrong path and needed no skip at all; two are
forgiven only for a specific MISSING line while every tracked digest stays hard;
and one was split so the half that needs no data keeps running. The objection
survives as a standing metric, which is why `clean_clone_gates.md` states the
skip share (4.8 %) and says outright that a skip added to silence a failure is
indistinguishable, in a summary line, from one that was always unrunnable.
