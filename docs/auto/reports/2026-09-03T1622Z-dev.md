## The root objection, and what it changed

sized to a count is a bank the student will not rehearse.** The document would cite
CHARTER §9 ("a lap that produces something the student could not explain in two minutes
produces a doc that makes it explainable") and implement its opposite — 30 essays are
more to explain, not less. The real failure mode is not "too few questions"; it is no
ranked subset, and no way to tell a drilled answer from an unbacked one at the booth.

First nail (cheaper than writing 30 answers and finding out): before writing, check each
candidate question mechanically for (a) a resolvable file path or registry key and
(b) distinctness from an existing question. Two design changes came out of it:
