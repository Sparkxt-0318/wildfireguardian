# Deferred routing extensions

These items are future work, not undisclosed changes to the frozen submission or prerequisites for using its bounded benchmark results. No further numerical campaign was launched during the submission freeze.

1. **Independent continuous-road proofs.** Validate radiation enclosures and lower-bound relationships independently, including between-sample behavior and the exact scope of global optimality/refusal. Sampling alone is not proof.
2. **Exact current-fire phases.** Replace the scalar maximum-remaining phase enclosure with preserved per-cell phases in a separate version, then repeat matched comparisons. The current enclosure is non-exact in 14/15 windows.
3. **Probability and source support.** Measure native receiver support along driving, waiting and stay; quantify the impact of explicit missing-probability zero assignment, unknown cumulative burned state and source-window truncation. Keep local ignition probability distinct from incoming radiation.
4. **Causal exposure histories.** Carry member-consistent incurred exposure from recorded earlier journeys, rather than interpreting supplied fixture values as validated history.
5. **Broader held-out evaluation.** Add independent fires, mission families, exposure-active journeys, hosts and repetitions, maintaining frozen outcomes and explicit unresolved cases. Do not count repeated windows as independent incidents.
6. **Latest-input policy ablation.** Separately evaluate contact, peak, dose and destination requirements on the same latest input identities. The completed historical ablation does not supply these latest-input causal estimates.
7. **Risk and time trade-offs.** Consider a separately labeled risk-ranking policy after no admissible route is established. Relaxed policies must not silently replace the current constraints or treat unresolved computation as infeasibility.
8. **Efficiency extensions.** Investigate checking cost, bounded memory and exact lower bounds only after profiling a frozen workload. Learning, frontier indexing, randomized search or kernel changes require total-cost and correctness evidence; adding a dependency is not a contribution by itself.
9. **Reproducibility and distribution.** Preserve upstream raw/training data custody, dependency artifacts and rights records, then test an isolated clean-machine setup. The new owner-local runtime companion preserves the current two trees but does not close every upstream custody obligation.
10. **Operational evaluation.** Test mobility, care requirements, destination suitability and live update behavior with intended users and responders. The elderly-focused social contribution remains a motivation, not a measured reduction in injuries or deaths.

## Submission decision

Freeze software 1.0.0, kit v3, checkpoint c651 and the evaluated inputs as the research reference. The accepted headline is **100% verified on-time resolution on the 20-mission benchmark**, with 15 checked-route missions and five scoped refusals. Retain the slower paired successful-route latency and inconsistent paired memory savings in the paper. Forecasting content remains for its author; earlier manuscript text before Section 5.4 is unchanged.
