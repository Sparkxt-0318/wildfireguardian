# Rehearsal records: procedure rehearsal, NOT evidence about people

These files come from AI agents playing readers and a coder. They hold no answers from any person.
- `rehearsal_A01_results.csv` (v2.2 columns and questions, kept as the record): the A01 agent reader's answers, coded by coder 1 (Clara) and coder 2 (an agent).
- `rehearsal_A01_scored.md`: the v2.2 `tools/score.py` applied to that sheet (v2.2 rules; superseded).
  - Its "decision" line is the scorer's text and means nothing here.
  - Q1 is unscoreable, because agents cannot be timed.
- `coder1_A01_v2.3.csv`: Clara's v2.3 codes of the same answers, written before coder 2's.
- `coder1_A01.csv`: Clara's codes, written before coder 2's were seen.

The B01 answers are summarised in `../REHEARSAL.md` and were not coded.
- `coder2_A01_v2.3.csv`: the coder-2 agent's v2.3 codes (from its report; it read only SCORING_SHEET.html as it stood before the two clarifications).
