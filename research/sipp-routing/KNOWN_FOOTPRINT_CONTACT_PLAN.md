# Proposed known-footprint contact certificate and bounded feasibility census

This is a proposal and read-only input audit, not an implemented support policy or a new routing study. The strict whole-domain default and its immutable zero-of-15,360 admission result remain unchanged. Unknown off-road cells alone do not rule out a contact-only certificate: contact is local to the cells actually occupied by a journey. Radiation is nonlocal, so this argument supplies no radiation, dose, peak-flux, full-policy or truth-risk certificate.

## Exact certificate being proposed

Let S be the selected original member identities, G the original pinned graph, and U_s the original unknown-cell mask for member s, augmented with invalid cutoff-state cells. Let I = [0,H] be the saved source clock relative to its exact cutoff, intersected with the original mission clock and every represented member time-validity bound. For a journey J, O(J) consists of the copied checker's closed cell/time occupancies: every directed chord interior subsegment with its midpoint cell, every grid-cut and endpoint with its floor cell, the initial position, legal node waits and destination dwell. Turns retain the incoming-edge identity and original forbidden-turn list. The current grammar has instantaneous node turns; it supplies no additional swept vehicle footprint or turn duration.

A proposed contact witness is admitted only when the original structure, continuity, lattice, direction, turn, destination-opening, dwell and horizon checks pass; every occupied (c,t) in O(J) lies within I and has c outside U_s for every selected s; and the copied independent occupancy checker finds no represented flame contact for any selected member on those spans. The check must use original masks and original source-bound member data after planning. A candidate from a derived support barrier is insufficient by itself.

The locality proof is direct. The contact predicate is a disjunction of member activity evaluated only on O(J). Two completions of unknown source data that agree on O(J) give the same contact predicate. Once all of O(J) is known and within source time support, changing unknown cells outside that footprint cannot change that predicate. This proves invariance only under the declared cell-local contact law, grid, chord grammar, scalar activity model and represented source values. It does not prove the model describes physical flames, whole-road width, observations or radiation.

The proposed candidate generator would pessimistically block an edge if any of its exact occupancies meets an unknown cell, and block unknown issue positions, node turns, waits and dwell. It would block every query time beyond source validity. Those are explicit support barriers in a separately versioned overlay, never filled source values or declarations of actual flame. Source graphs, raw arrays, masks, mission thresholds and defaults remain byte-preserved. The common-mask proof means spatial barriers can be shared across all 1,024 saved identities without creating or replicating a trajectory.

A returned witness could be labelled CHECKED_CONTACT_ROUTE_WITHIN_KNOWN_FOOTPRINT after independent checking. A failed restricted search must be UNRESOLVED_RESTRICTED_SUPPORT, including a disconnected restricted topology or an exhausted search. It cannot establish global NO_ROUTE or global earliest arrival in the original unknown domain. Any arrival optimum is at most conditional on the support-restricted problem and must be named accordingly. Original issued-history fields remain evidence; no incurred history is fabricated or reassigned to new member identities. Contact-only timings are not evidence that the unchanged dose/peak history passes.

## Time support and represented activity assumptions

The actual saved exports have a one-hour horizon. Their -1 value means no first ignition through that saved horizon under the trajectory construction, not global never. Infinity inside a contact representation must carry this finite validity bound. A witness finishing after 3,600 seconds, including its full dwell, is unscorable under these sources even if every visited cell is spatially known. The terminal source point is admitted only if the declared representation explicitly supports it; no final interval is silently extrapolated.

Positive first-burn hours require the declared sub-hour timing rule and seed; the existing uniform rule places ignition within that first hour. Activity is the represented half-open interval [b,b+T), evaluated only within source validity. The scalar T, initial active set and remaining-time enclosure must be recorded from the existing source/construction assumptions. Unknown initial age on a known burned cell can use the explicitly declared full-T pessimistic enclosure, with its assumption disclosed. It cannot be described as observed exact ignition/extinction. Neither ordered timing nor a longer residence time establishes future ignition support after the saved horizon. No new ignition-time draws or contact model are constructed in this topology audit.

## Read-only feasibility census

`audit_known_footprint_topology.py` binds all 20 original Korean missions, five original graph files, 15 exact existing export domains and the complete saved-N shared-mask proof. It reads only original slot0 for its mask and verifies that mask against the immutable full-N result; it does not treat that slot as a newly replicated member. It also retains the separate cutoff-state validity mask. Exact source NPZ/record, graph/request/native-metadata, source affine/EPSG, inclusive existing regional window and cutoff/as-of identities are checked. Existing raw arrays are never resampled or filled.

For each domain the audit counts known nodes, known waitable nodes and known directed edges under the full chord/end/cut-point footprint. A closed cell rectangle containing a chord is a sufficient all-known shortcut: every chord point lies between the represented endpoint coordinates, and coordinate floor is monotone. If that rectangle contains an unknown cell, the original exact Fraction occupancy routine decides support. Unknown endpoints immediately block an edge because endpoint point-contact is part of the copied grammar. A tiny exhaustive fixture compares this shortcut to that exact routine for all 512 masks of a 3x3 grid, across 1,536 edge/mask pairs, including boundary-point cells; incoming turn restrictions and outside-node refusal are checked separately.

Static reachability keeps incoming-edge states and the original forbidden turns. Its edge witnesses certify only the existence of a directed, spatially known topology from the original issue node to a destination node. It ignores flames, path travel costs, waiting and temporal arrival feasibility. The audit separately records the unchanged destination openings/dwell and their intersection with source validity. A positive topology result is not a timed route or a contact pass; a negative result describes only this conservative known-footprint restriction. Static predecessor exploration is not a sweep/SIPP execution or a resource/performance benchmark.

## Conditional next study, requiring review first

Only after the census and this certificate are reviewed would a separately versioned opt-in contact adapter be implemented and tested. Meaningful tests must cover unknown cells on chord interiors and grid-cut points, known/unknown issue positions and destinations, instantaneous turns and incoming restrictions, wait/dwell support, exact horizon equality and beyond-horizon refusal, right-censored -1 values, immutable raw masks, independent witness checking, and restricted-exhaustion mapping to unresolved. Both planners must consume the same support barriers and dynamic contact law. The strict adapter must keep its current behavior.

If admitted supported topology exists and the adapter passes those checks, the intended scaling panel is the fixed 20 Korean missions and original saved first-K identities 0..K-1 at K = 4,8,16,32, with no member replication or missing-mass deletion. The source horizon, original mission clocks/windows/dwell and declared uniform timing seed must be fixed before execution. Paired sweep/SIPP would use two ABBA repetitions and the original 30 s/3,072 MiB limits under HEAVY, reporting each solver's distinct state/work counters, found/exhausted/timeout outcomes, and independent contact checks. Off-road unknowns remain stored. This conditional protocol does not authorize that adapter or campaign now.

Road-arrival reduction features could be evaluated on the same geometrically restricted known road edges while retaining all N scenario identities and masses. Their never/after-horizon cap must mean no unsafe departure inside the stated finite domain, not global never. Existing full-grid L1 and road-symmetric-difference constructors still reject unknown source fields; this proposal does not relabel those distances as supported or discard unknown empirical mass. Contact scaling alone cannot complete the separately gated reduction, thermal or scientific risk studies.
