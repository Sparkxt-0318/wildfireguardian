## Decisions

- Export v3 is an overlay `experimental/scenario-members/export_v3.py` (orchestrator decision D1); `integration/src/wfg_latest/export.py` stays byte-identical. Two modes: `model` (full v2 export plus `simulate` with `trajectory_callback`, needs the environment) and `replay` (one-hour exact trajectories from a frozen v2 export, needs PyTorch only for the identical random streams). The 15 Korean windows are exported in replay mode; the model mode is tested on a synthetic fixture.
- Bridge v2 reuses the frozen bridge and packet writer unchanged by injecting the trajectory ignition seconds at the same point where `packets.construct_regional` injects the full-event draw, then records `dependence = "trajectory"`, `timing`, `member_selection` and `acceptance` in the packet settings.
- SIPP must handle non-waitable nodes (most road nodes). Classic SIPP assumes waiting is allowed in every configuration; here a state at a non-waitable head carries an interval of admissible arrival seconds instead of a single earliest label, and dominance is set inclusion. At waitable heads the label is the earliest arrival into the safe interval (Phillips and Likhachev). This is documented in `docs/SIPP_EQUIVALENCE.md` and tested against the frozen sweep and a brute-force lattice search.
- Planner used for evidence in 1C-1: the frozen contact sweep (exact relaxation) plus the frozen centre checker per member, so that 1C-1 does not depend on 1C-2. 1C-2 provides SIPP; 1C-3 uses SIPP and imports 1C-1 (members, reduction, acceptance) and 1C-2 (planner), which its README states.
- Chance-constrained (`k_of_m`) search: exact subset enumeration with two prunings: the shared present-fire part is contained in every subset (sandwich bound between the shared-only and the all-member arrival), and members whose new ignitions touch no road occupancy are free.
- Truths: `holdout_trajectory` (trajectories withheld from the selected members), `reconstructed_arrival` (Zenodo TOA rasters, new ignitions at their reconstructed second within the hour). `observed_perimeters` (NIROPS) cover only U.S. fires that are not in this panel, and `external_simulator` needs FARSITE, which is not installed; both are skipped and reported as such.
- Unresolved is kept as its own outcome in every table. Repetitions are never counted as independent cases, and the two Gyeongbuk panels (Uiseong-Andong and Yeongdeok, both KFS_01) are one parent fire.
