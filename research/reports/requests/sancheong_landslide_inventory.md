Recipient: the Nguyen, Song and Kim group at Pukyong National University

Purpose: WildfireGuardian is a Korea-focused research program studying

hypothesis and will not be redistributed.

**Subject: Request to use the Sancheong 2025 landslide inventory (568 initiation points) in a Korean wildfire-landslide research program**

Dear Dr. Nguyen, Dr. Song and Dr. Kim,

We are writing about your paper on the Sancheong 2025 landslide event
(Landslides, volume 23, pages 2363 to 2381), which documents 568 landslide
initiation points.

We are a small Korea-focused research program studying whether a burned
Korean forest slope stays more prone to landslides for some bounded period
after a wildfire, and whether that period differs between pine-dominated and
broadleaf-dominated stands. This is an open hypothesis we are testing, not a
result we have already reached. The Sancheong 2025 event is one of a small
number of Korean cases where a wildfire and a landslide are documented close
together in time and place, which makes your inventory unusually valuable for
this specific question.

We would like to ask whether we could obtain the initiation-point locations
(and any associated attributes you are able to share, such as date, area or
slope) from your inventory, for use in a non-commercial Korean research
program. We are a small team, working on public and low-cost data only, with
no funding for paid data acquisition.

If you are able to share the data, we would be glad to describe exactly how
it would be used (a statistical model of post-fire landslide susceptibility
over Korean terrain) and to agree to any conditions you set. We will not
redistribute the data to any other party, and we will cite your paper as the
source in any resulting report or publication.

Thank you for considering this request, and for the inventory itself, which
is a valuable contribution on its own.

Sincerely,
[sender name and affiliation to be filled in before sending]
