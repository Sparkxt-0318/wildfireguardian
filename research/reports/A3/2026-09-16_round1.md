## 6. The design in one paragraph

A segment of forest road, river or ridge, at most 100 m long and at most 30
degrees of bearing change, is an **encounter** when the windward side is burned up
to the barrier, the windward burn is connected to the main fire, an active-fire
detection places the front there, and the encounter can be timed. An encounter is
**held** when the lee-side burned fraction in a band 10 m to 50 m from the barrier
edge is below 0.10, **crossed** when it is at or above 0.50, touches the barrier
edge and survives a flanking attribution test, and **indeterminate** otherwise,
always with a reason code and never dropped. Breach probability is modelled as
`1 - (1 - flame crossing)(1 - ember spotting)`, the flame arm a Bayesian
hierarchical logistic in effective width, slope direction, intensity, lee fuel and
arrival-hour weather with a fire-complex random intercept, the ember arm a
truncated jump-distance distribution with a Poisson spotting rate. Validation is
leave-one-complex-out over five folds, primary metric the mean out-of-complex log
predictive score, and the model must beat a width-only logistic regression in at
least 4 of 5 folds by at least 0.02 nats per segment or that baseline becomes the
model of record.

## 7. Open problems, in the order they threaten the study

### 7.1 The width covariate has no source, and the error biases toward the headline

A1 confirmed that the forest road SHP has no width attribute. Width must therefore
be measured from pre-fire orthoimagery, and measurement error in a covariate
attenuates its slope toward zero, which pushes the study toward reporting that
width does not matter. That is the study's own most quotable negative finding, so
an uncorrected analysis would be biased toward manufacturing it.

What the design now does about it: an errors-in-variables term with bias and error
standard deviation estimated jointly from at least 60 hand-measured segments, the
attenuation factor reported as a headline diagnostic, gate M1 blocking publication
of any breach curve if the attenuation factor falls below 0.70, and falsification
condition F1 judged only on the corrected posterior. When M1 is not met the outcome
is written as **not resolvable at the achieved measurement precision**, which is a
different sentence from "width does not matter".

### 7.2 Width support: there may be no contrast to regress against

Korean forest road design classes cluster well below 6 m. If the canopy gap has
little spread, there is nothing to estimate, and a width slope carried by the
road-versus-river contrast is a barrier-type effect wearing a width effect's
clothes. Gate W1 requires at least 25 road segments on each side of 6 m before a
width curve is published. This compounds with 7.1: a small true width spread is
exactly the condition under which a given measurement error does the most damage.

### 7.3 Suppression anchoring has no data fix in this round

A road that held partly measures that a crew was anchored on it. There is no
suppression resource placement record in the registry, and getting one is an
information-disclosure request. The estimand is therefore stated as associational
and operational, and no output attributes the estimate to the physical gap alone.
New human gate proposed below.

### 7.4 Detection cadence is confounded with the fold unit

NOAA-21 only covers the 2025 fires, so timing and intensity are measured more
finely in 2025 than in 2019, and the fire that supplies the cadence is also the
fold and the random-effect level. Mitigation is a pre-registered common-cadence
run degrading every fire to Suomi NPP plus NOAA-20, believed over the primary if
the two disagree.

### 7.5 The smoke mask is undefined

Section 5.2 requires one and does not specify it. Q5 in `OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`. It
must close before the signature, because undetected smoke biases dNBR low, which
biases segments toward `held`.

### 7.6 Sample size may put the study in the G3 branch

Order 100 labelable segments is the working expectation, with held segments much
rarer than crossed ones because held segments sit on the perimeter while crossed
ones fill the interior. Below 60 labelable or 10 held, no model is fitted and the
round delivers the labelled dataset and the descriptive accounting instead.

Section 6.5's effective-width formulation is attributed to Swedosh et al. 2021 and
is explicitly blocked on A7 confirming the formulation. All five prior-art items in
section 14 are flagged unverified by A3.
