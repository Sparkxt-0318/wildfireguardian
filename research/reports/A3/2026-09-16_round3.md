## The headline answers

**The new cutoff, and the rate it is set from.** The side statistic is
`T = s / se(s)` where `se(s)` is estimated from the residuals of a plane carrying
three parameters in two dimensions, so `T` is Student's t on `nu = n_det - 3`.
The cutoff is now

    t_crit = t_{1 - alpha}( n_det - 3 ),   evaluated per segment

at a pre-registered **one-sided false-assignment rate of `alpha` = 0.01 per tier**,
with a **union bound of 0.02** across the two tiers by Bonferroni, and a declared
grid of {0.005, 0.01, 0.025} stated in `alpha` and never in `z`. `alpha` is defined
exactly: it is the supremum over the null boundary of the per-segment probability
that the rule names the **opposite** side, attained only as the true noncentrality
goes to zero. At the C4 minimum of 5 detections the cutoff is **6.97**, not 2.0.

A6's table reproduces exactly under an independent re-run: 0.0918, 0.0697, 0.0510,
0.0383, 0.0290, 0.0264 per tail at `n_det` of 5, 6, 8, 12, 25, 40 against the 0.0228
that 2.0 implies, and at two degrees of freedom 2.5 gives 0.0648 and 1.5 gives
0.1362. The grid was on the wrong axis.

**Why 0.01.** Three reasons, all in v0.3 section 5.1.2a. A wrong side removes a
**hold**, so the rate is a tax on the rare class, and a union of 0.02 keeps the
expected number of holds destroyed below one at gate G1's threshold, which is inside
the three-in-thirty tolerance gate L1 already accepts. A smaller `alpha` does not
remove the leak, it moves it: every segment a stricter cutoff saves from mislabelling
exits `SIDE` instead, and the `SIDE` exclusion is outcome dependent in the same
hold-removing direction and is harder to see. And 0.02 across two tiers lands within
a rounding of the 0.023 per tail that v0.2's 2.0 was evidently meant to buy, so the
change reads as a corrected instrument rather than a new ambition smuggled in with a
fix. **The level itself is now Q12, open, and A6 decides**, because a level chosen by
one agent should not survive unexamined into a frozen document.

**What the correction costs, stated rather than buried.** The recomputed angle table
is much harsher than v0.2's. At `n_det` of 5 and a front irregularity of 0.15 the
minimum resolvable approach angle moves from 15.6 degrees to **69.1 degrees**, and at
0.20 it does not resolve at any angle at all. The new `c_max` row gives the largest
irregularity resolvable at any angle: 0.16 at `n_det` of 5, 0.27 at 6, 0.42 at 8,
0.61 at 12, 1.00 at 25. **The bottom of the detection range has stopped
contributing**, gate S1 is more likely to fire, and that is the design failing safe:
those segments were previously being bought at four times the wrong-side rate the
document believed it was paying.

**What the hold-removing error does to the ratio.** The mechanism is v0.3 section
5.1.5. A swap puts C1, the windward-burned test, on the true lee band. On a crossing
that is harmless, because both bands burned. On a hold it is fatal: C1 fails and the
segment exits `NOBURN`. **Side error removes holds and leaves crossings.** With `q`
the realised union pass fraction that gate S1 requires to be at least 0.25,

    R_observed / R_true  >=  1 - alpha_union / q

| regime | per-tail wrong-side rate | worst-case depression of held:crossed at `q` = 0.25 |
|---|---|---|
| v0.3, one tier | 0.01 | 0.04 |
| v0.3, both tiers | 0.02 | **0.08** |
| v0.2's 2.0 at `nu` of 2, one tier | 0.0918 | **0.37** |

So the fix takes the worst case on the rare class from about a third of the held
class down to under a tenth. The realised figure is far below the bound, because the
wrong-side probability collapses once the true side is real at all: at `alpha` = 0.01
and a noncentrality of 1.0 it is already 0.0015 at two degrees of freedom and 0.0005
at twenty-two; at 2.0 it is below 0.0001 everywhere. The bound is attained only where
there is nothing to get right, and those segments mostly exit `SIDE` instead.

**Does it bias the breach curve?** Three different answers, and separating them is
the part that matters.

1. **The level moves.** Removing holds and not crossings shifts the intercept toward
   a higher breach probability at every width. In a logistic model, sampling on the
   outcome alone moves the intercept and leaves the slope alone, so this is the
   largest effect and the least damaging to the coefficient of interest.
2. **The slope in `W_cleared` is unbiased to first order, conditional on one thing
   that is now measured.** The wrong-side probability is a function of approach
   angle, detection count and front irregularity. None of those is `W_cleared`. So
   unless approach angle is associated with cleared width, the loss is
   non-differential and F3, which is stated in `W_cleared`, is protected. Check S0
   now reports that rank correlation at `checks.S0.theta_vs_wcleared_rho`, before any
   label exists, with the reading written in advance.
3. **The slope in `v = log(W_eff + 1)` is attenuated.** The wrong-side probability
   rises as `sin(theta)` falls, and so does `W_eff`, so holds are lost preferentially
   at the wide end of the fitted covariate. That pulls the slope toward zero, which
   under `H-ROADS` is the conservative direction. This route cannot manufacture the
   result the hypothesis predicts.

**The residual, and it is real.** The ratio arithmetic assumes the noncentrality
distribution is the same for holds and crossings. It is not, in exactly the direction
condition C3 exists to measure: a stopped front leaves a kinked arrival surface,
which is a larger irregularity, which is both a higher wrong-side probability and a
higher exclusion rate. If that excess is itself associated with width, item 2 stops
protecting the slope. **That is why the C3 check is stratified by width tercile and
not pooled**, which is A3's addition to A6's specification and the one thing in it
that bears on the slope rather than on the intercept.

**Do I agree that gate W1 fails before gate M1? Partly, and I declined to predict.**
A6's mechanism is right and A3 accepts every step of it except the last. The
**lower** truncation, `NOEXIST`, acts on `W_cleared` directly, and W1 counts in
`W_cleared` by A6's own ruling, so that one reaches W1. The **upper** truncation acts
on `W_eff`, through `sin(theta)`, and approach angle is a property of the front's
bearing against the barrier's bearing rather than of the road's design class. It
reaches `Var(v_true)`, hence `kappa_v`, hence **M1**. It reaches W1 only through the
`theta` to `W_cleared` association, which nobody has measured. **So the
squeezed-from-both-ends picture is a statement about `kappa_v` and gate M1, and gate
W1 is squeezed from below only.**

A3 does not conclude from that that M1 fails first. The point is that **neither
ordering is derivable**, because the `NOEXIST` rate depends on a ground sample
distance nobody has and the `theta` to `W_cleared` association is an empirical fact
about Korean forest roads that nobody has measured. So v0.3 adds **check W0** in
section 11.3: a pre-labelling projection, on the R-set, of W1's 6 m counts and M1's
own input. W0's counts are an upper bound on W1's, since labelling only removes
segments, so a W0 failure is a W1 failure and a W0 pass implies nothing. Together
with check S0's `theta_vs_wcleared_rho`, A6's prediction and A3's qualification are
both scoreable against numbers that exist before any labelling effort is spent, and
both were written before either number exists. The task asked the document to report
what actually happens rather than assert which gate fails first, and that is the
shape the answer took.

## The two pieces of code, and what they buy beyond the document

Both run today. Both ship no data. Both are exercised only on synthetic geometry
generated in-process from a declared seed, and the pre-registration and the test
files say so in terms: **no number from either is a measurement and none may be
quoted anywhere.**

**`s0_side_check.py`.** The section 5.1.3 tables are now generated rather than typed,
so A6's verification action for C2, re-deriving the angle table from the declared
`alpha`, is `python research/roads/s0_side_check.py --tables` rather than a
transcription check. The tests do three things a paragraph cannot: they pin A6's
per-tail table to five decimal places, they pin the recomputed angle table and the
`c_max` row to the values printed in the document, and they **demonstrate the
calibration by Monte Carlo** rather than asserting it. Under a front with a zero
projected gradient at five detections, the corrected cutoff assigns a side at about
`2 * alpha` and a fixed 2.0 assigns at more than three times that. There is also a
structural test that check S0 imports no raster or vector reader and calls nothing
that opens a path, which is the section 6.1 provenance firewall as a test rather than
a promise.

**`recoverability.py`.** The geometry object refuses to exist without naming which of
the three sets it is, which is condition C4 enforced by a constructor rather than by
a convention. The pre-registered NUTS backend raises `NotImplementedError` naming
WJ-009 and WJ-001 rather than silently falling back, and a `laplace` number carries
`gate_readable: false` and can never be read by gate R0 or gate M2. On synthetic
geometry the harness reproduces A6's finding directly: truncating grazing angles
lowers `Var(v_true)` and lowers the mean squared measurement error at the same time,
and detection power computed on the candidate set comes out materially above the same
figure on the R-set. That is the defect C4 closes, made visible instead of argued.

**One thing the harness surfaced that went back into the document.** The probability
contrast between 3 m and 6 m of `W_cleared` is bounded above at any slope, because
`v(3 m)` and `v(6 m)` are close on the log scale and a large slope of either sign
saturates both probabilities. The harness raises rather than clipping when a
requested effect is unreachable at the simulation's intercept, and section 11.5.2 now
says that an unreachable smallest effect of interest is a reportable design finding
rather than a harness error, with the largest attainable contrast written beside the
requested one.

## Where A3 reads A6's conditions differently

Both are in v0.3 section 17.5. **Both conditions are satisfied as written.** Neither
reading changes anything the version does, and neither is a rebuttal under
`SIGNOFF.md` section 5.

**1. The W1-before-M1 prediction rests on a step that does not carry.** Set out
above. The upper truncation acts on `W_eff` and reaches M1; W1 counts in `W_cleared`
by A6's own ruling and is reached by the lower truncation only. A3's response is not
a counter-prediction but check W0 and `checks.S0.theta_vs_wcleared_rho`, so the
question is settled by two numbers that exist before any labelling.

**2. C4's scope needs one sentence it does not have: the retained set is not the
fitted set.** The R-set is everything determinable before the scenes are read, and
section 5.7 removes more segments afterwards, at five further steps, in a way section
12.9 already expects to be outcome dependent. So an R-set instrument still overstates
the power of the fit that runs, by less than a candidate-set instrument and by more
than nothing. The gates must fire before labelling or gate R0 has no purpose, so this
cannot be fixed by moving them. Section 5.1.6 adds a third, non-gating value computed
on the labelled set and reported beside the other two, which turns the residual
overstatement into a measured quantity. **That is an addition to C4, not a
qualification of it.**

Nothing else in A6's ten conditions reads as wrong to A3. C3 in particular is the
best finding in the record: the unordered pair is side invariant, it measures the
thing section 12.9 could not reach, and it costs nothing because the accounting
script already has both bands' burned fractions. A3 should have found it and did not.
