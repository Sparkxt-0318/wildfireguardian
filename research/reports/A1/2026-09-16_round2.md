## T2.7: forest road attribute schema, and A3's two questions

**No attribute-level schema table can be produced this round.** The SHP was
never downloaded (see above), and data.go.kr does not publish a
column-by-column data dictionary for this listing anywhere reachable
without downloading the file: the "표준 메타데이터" tab on the portal page
is DCAT/Schema.org boilerplate (cost, licence, reuse scope), not a field
list, and no separate 데이터명세서 (data specification) file is attached to
the listing.

The only description of contents available without the file is the
portal's marketing blurb (both the `og:description` meta tag and the
"자료설명" field, word-for-word identical):

> "산림청이 제공하는 공간정보 데이터로, 전국 산림 내에 조성된 임도의 위치와
> 연결망 현황을 지도 형태로 시각화한 자료입니다. 이 데이터는 임도의 **노선,
> 위치 좌표, 구간 정보, 연결 구조** 등을 포함하고 있으며..."

That names four attribute *categories*, not field names: route line,
coordinate position, section information, connection structure. Nothing
else. Given that, here is the honest table:

| field name | dtype | example value | null fraction | what it appears to mean |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| (unknown -- route/노선) | unknown | unknown | unknown | likely a route or line-segment identifier; category name only, no field name given |
| (unknown -- 위치 좌표) | unknown | unknown | unknown | coordinate geometry, presumably the SHP's own geometry column, not a separate attribute |
| (unknown -- 구간 정보) | unknown | unknown | unknown | section/segment info; could plausibly carry a road-class subfield, cannot confirm |
| (unknown -- 연결 구조) | unknown | unknown | unknown | how segments connect into a network; could plausibly carry junction attributes, cannot confirm |

**A3's question 1, road class field (간선임도/지선임도/작업임도):** not
confirmed either way. The blurb's category names do not mention it by name,
but "구간 정보" (section information) is vague enough that it could contain
a class code without the blurb saying so. This cannot be resolved without
either the file itself or a direct question to KFS.

**A3's question 2, construction or survey year field:** also not confirmed,
but the evidence leans toward **absent**. The blurb lists four content
categories and none of them is temporal. The only dates associated with
this dataset are dataset-level, not per-road: the filename carries
`20200914`, and the portal's "최근 수정일" showed `2025-11-20` at the time
of the round-1 check. Both describe when the *layer as a whole* was last
touched or republished, not when any individual road segment was built or
last surveyed. **If that holds once the file is actually read, A3's
concern is real and specific: a 2023-built road cannot be told apart from
a 1998 one inside this file, which means the file alone cannot tell you
whether a given road existed on the day of a given fire.** The only two
things in reach that could still date a road, neither confirmed to exist
in this file and neither substitutable for the other:

- A construction-year attribute might exist in the file despite not being
  named in the blurb (blurbs on this portal are consistently generic
  marketing copy, not specs, as seen on every other dataset checked this
  round and last).
- Failing that, dating individual roads would fall to the pre-fire
  orthoimagery direction (T2.9): a road visible in imagery confirmed to
  predate a fire is itself evidence the road existed by that date, which
  is exactly why WJ-009 (per-tile vintage) matters as much as the width
  question it was originally raised for.

**Bottom line for A3: do not build the encounter dataset assuming a
construction-year field exists.** Whether it can be built at all is
undetermined until either (a) the SHP is obtained (blocked on the
consent-gate human decision below) or (b) per-tile-dated pre-fire imagery
is obtained (blocked on WJ-009) and used to date roads independently of
the vector file.
