## 3. What was measured, and what was deliberately not

`design/clock_and_address.py` reads the verified statistics CSV and touches
neither 피해면적_합계 nor any duration. Its docstring says so and says why. Four
findings, all reproducible in one command.

**The clock is sound.** Report times are minute-resolved; 2.48 per cent fall on
the hour. There is no rounding artefact, so an hourly discrete-time hazard has a
clock that can carry it. This is the only thing this round that came back better
than feared.

**The night stratum is thin at the fire level.** 1,576 of 2,020 fires are
reported between 10:00 and 17:59 KST. 210, which is 10.40 per cent, are reported
in the clock-hour night. **The night arm's exposure in the hazard model is not
that number**, because the model counts fire-hours and fire-hours depend on
durations, which are the outcome. So it cannot be known before the fit, and
pre-registration section 12.5 makes reporting it a condition on the arm's
output rather than letting it be skipped.

**The address ceiling is the same one the landslides direction hit.** Zero of the
2,019 rows carrying a finest address field carry a lot number in it. The finest
reachable geocoding rung is a 리 or 동 polygon. Two further defects that a naive
join would hit silently: 191 of the 1,250 distinct finest names occur under more
than one parent path, so resolution must be on the full parent path; and the
province column carries 18 raw spellings for 17 provinces because one row spells
충남 with a trailing space.

**The cause covariate the brief specified is not a first-hours covariate, and the
record says so in its own words.** 1,479 of 2,020 rows, 73.22 per cent, carry the
free-text escape value in the coded detail column. The free-text field carries
조사중, 추정 or 미상 on **702 rows, 34.75 per cent**, and some rows read 조사중
verbatim, which means "under investigation". A field that records its own
investigation status is the outcome of an enquiry, not an observation available
three hours after a report. Cause is removed from the primary model and survives
only as a named, unpromotable secondary.

**Deliberately not measured:** the burned-area distribution at any threshold, the
report-to-containment duration in any form, and any cross-tabulation of a
covariate against either. Those are this direction's two outcome variables.

## 4. The five things the brief asked to be confronted

### 4.1 The counterfactual, and why it is worse than "thin data"

**The word "censoring" is doing damage and the pre-registration drops it.** In
ordinary right-censoring, identification rests on having subjects who experience
the event. Here every Korean forest fire is suppressed, so the event indicator is
zero on every row in the record. **There is no subject with the event.** The
record contains not one Korean fire that burned to its own natural end, and any
estimate of the unsuppressed distribution is whatever the model extrapolates past
the last instant any fire was observed burning. **This does not improve with a
longer record.** Thirty more years would add thirty more years of suppressed
fires.

**The estimand is therefore split in two.** Q-CF, the counterfactual, is
withdrawn as a reported quantity. Q-OP, the probability that a fire's **recorded**
final size reaches a floor under the regime that operated, is identified, is what
the record contains, and is all the brief's deliverable needs. An integer point
score that flags fires in their first hours is a statement about Q-OP.

### 4.2 Four years, and the gate

**Gate G1 is the registered split itself.** It refuses the committed record today
and A6's own committed test asserts that it does. The pre-registration takes the
refusal message's second option, which is to say the record is too short, and
explicitly declines the first, which is to shorten the training requirement. A
modeller who shortens a registered training window because it refuses his data
has converted a gate into a preference.

Five further gates sit behind it, each with its numbers fixed in the document:
an exceedance count per fold, a concentration limit so that one complex cannot
carry a fold, a simulation-based recoverability instrument with a declared
operating characteristic, a floor before the tail shape may carry covariates at
all, and the neural model held closed.

### 4.3 The report clock, which is the direction's real weak point

Section 5 of the pre-registration works the algebra. The fitted log-hazard picks
up a term equal to the covariate's report offset multiplied by the age slope of
the true hazard. The age slope is negative in any plausible containment process,
so a covariate with a larger offset acquires a lower fitted containment hazard,
therefore a longer fitted duration, therefore a larger fitted size, **from the
clock alone**.

The covariates with large offsets are the remote ones and the night ones. Those
are the access-distance covariate and the night indicator. **Both of the
direction's headline covariates are biased by the clock in the direction that
confirms the hypothesis**, which is the same shape as the reporting-attention
finding A6 recorded against the landslides direction.

The response is a sixteen-cell sensitivity grid with the naive cell reported as
primary so the assumption is visible, a withdrawal rule if a sign flips anywhere
inside it, and measurement M-R: for fires with a satellite detection timestamped
before their reported start, the difference is an observed lower bound on the
offset. M-R is Korean, public and low-cost, and it is behind the same key as
everything else.

### 4.4 The held-out year

Four mechanisms by which a held-out report year is not independent of its
training years, including one the brief did not name: the `forward_chaining_by_year`
docstring states that this direction needs a year-boundary embargo and the
registered kwargs set it to zero. The pre-registration does not change A6's
registry; it registers a row-level boundary rule that closes the same hole
without costing a training year, and `OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` Q3 asks A6 which
reading is intended.

### 4.5 The neural extreme-value model

Not designed against, not in the fit enumeration, and not reserved for. Three
reasons, and the third settles it: **it is not in the verified bibliography.**
A method this direction cannot cite is a method it cannot pre-register.

## 6. The one number that decides this direction

The registered scheme yields `max(0, Y - 10)` folds on `Y` distinct report
years. So:

**The Korean record must reach back to 2015 for a single fold to exist, and to
2010 for the season ledger to mean anything. A record that stops at 2016 is
worth no more to this direction than the CSV already on disk.**

That is the sentence for whoever clears WJ-001, and it was not obvious before the
arithmetic was run. It is in `DATA_REQUIREMENTS.md` section 0, ahead of every
other dataset, because the other nine change what the direction can measure and
this one decides whether it measures anything.

## 8. A5's own judgement

**On a four-year record this direction should not fit anything, and nothing in
what A5 wrote permits it.** Gate G1 is shut by someone else's code with someone
else's test on it, which is the right kind of gate to be stopped by.

**The direction should still proceed**, on the same three grounds A6 accepted for
landslides. The outcome-free work is real and part of it is already done: the
cause-field finding alone removes a covariate the brief assumed was available,
and it cost one script. The data case turns WJ-001 from a blocker into a decision
with a stated payoff and a stated threshold year. And the gate structure means
the direction can be handed a longer record and run without renegotiating
anything.

**The weakest point is not the counterfactual.** That quantity is withdrawn and a
withdrawn quantity cannot be wrong. The weakest point is the report clock, and
specifically that its mitigation is a grid whose endpoints are guesses. A5's
recommendation to A6 is `OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` Q8's route B: demote the night arm
and the access-distance coefficient to permanently secondary status in v0.1,
rather than making them hostage to a satellite key that may never arrive.

**What A5 expects to happen.** Failing condition F2, more likely than every other
outcome combined: the key does not arrive, or it arrives and the record does not
reach 2015, gate G1 stays shut, and this direction's deliverable is the
accounting rather than a model. That is a real output of a round and it is the
honest one.
