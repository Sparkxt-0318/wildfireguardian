## 2. What A3's three new sections are worth

I was told not to accept a section because it exists. I did not, and two of the
three hold up.

**Section 11.5, the flat-curve reading rule: accepted, with two repairs.** This
is the best-argued thing in the program so far. Three of its five instruments run
before any label exists, which is the property that makes it a solution rather
than a disclosure, and instrument 5, separating a noisy ruler from a broken one,
is something I did not ask for and would not have specified in that form. The two
repairs are technical: `kappa` and gate M1 are computed for a covariate entering
linearly, while the covariate that enters the model is `log(W_eff + 1)` with
`W_eff = W_cleared / sin(theta)`, so the attenuation is heteroscedastic and
segment-dependent and the gate is set on the wrong scale; and instrument 2
measures detection power under a non-null while instrument 4 gates the
informative-null claim on the ability to **exclude** the smallest effect of
interest, which is a different quantity and is not simulated anywhere.

**Section 7.7, unidentifiability: accepted.** It names the right parameter, gives
the structural reason no amount of the same data fixes it, refuses the dishonest
fix of a tighter prior, names the different-support evidence that would identify
it, and fixes in advance the four things reported if it stays unidentified,
including renaming the flame arm's coefficients as coefficients of a combined
breach model in the abstract. One drafting defect: F4 fires on "the ember jump
parameters" while the parameter section 7.3 expects to fail is the rate
`lambda`, so F4 may not fire in the case it exists for.

**Section 12.4, suppression: accepted as far as it goes, which is not far
enough.** The mechanism is stated in its specific form, the proximity proxy is
refused for the right reason, and "operational barrier performance" is a real
scope cut rather than a rename, because it forecloses the counterfactual reading

accepted scope cut rather than `unresolved`. Two gaps. The vocabulary is enforced

of day as a candidate mitigation. That contrast costs nothing and must be
pre-registered with both its outcomes written down before it is run.

## 3. The acceptance tests I ran, and what they returned

- **The `SIDE` rule (section 5.1)** assigns the windward side by comparing the

## 4. The rulings A3 asked for

**The smallest effect of interest: not ratified at 0.10. Set at 0.05, and restate
the unit.** The unit is wrong before the level is: the effect is stated over a
3 m to 9 m contrast in **effective** width, which is a composite of width and
approach angle, so at a median angle of 45 degrees it is a 2.1 m to 6.4 m
contrast in cleared width and at the 15 degree clip it is 0.8 m to 2.3 m. A
planner builds cleared metres and the claim under test is stated in built metres,
so the contrast must be stated in `W_cleared` at the sample median approach
angle. On the level, I rule 0.05 for three reasons: the threshold must be set by
what matters rather than by what is reachable, which is the entire point of
instrument 3; the decision it serves is the capital one, which aggregates over
hundreds of encounters, rather than the single-incident one, which is not
sensitive at either level; and in a life-safety tool the error costs are
asymmetric, so declaring an effect "not worth acting on" at 0.10 leaves 0.09 on
the table. I expect 0.05 to be unreachable on the planned sample, and I said so
in the record in advance: that is the correct outcome, it is cheaper to learn it
before the labelling than after, and it is exactly what instrument 2 exists to
deliver. Report the equivalence reading at both 0.05 and 0.10, with 0.05 primary.

**Recoverability at 0.80: ratified as a level.** Report the achieved value, not a
pass or fail, and add the zero-slope exclusion-power arm.

**Attenuation gate at `kappa` of 0.70: ratified as a level, refused as applied.**
Gate on the lower end of a declared interval rather than a point estimate, since
30 repeat pairs give `sigma_u` to 13 per cent relative and a point gate at 0.70
is passed by noise half the time when the truth is 0.70. And define it on the
covariate that enters the model.
