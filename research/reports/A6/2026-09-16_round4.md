## 5. The findings that are new this round

Six, none needing data.

1. **Section 7.1's identification claim is not true of the model section 10.3
   registers.** With unburned controls in the risk set and a calendar main
   effect, the deficiency is one and both the linear years-since-fire term and
   its curvature are identified; with free cohort dummies it is two and both
   remain identified; it reaches five only when a burned-by-calendar-year
   interaction is free. So the estimate is carried by the assumption that the
   calendar effect is the same on burned and unburned ground, and section 6.5's
   own reporting-attention mechanism violates exactly that assumption with the
   same sign and time profile as the hypothesis. Section 7.6's M-A reading rule
   is therefore calibrated against a matrix the design does not have. Condition
   C2, and the kill shot's test T1 is built on it.
2. **The F4 margin is on the wrong scale.** 0.01 nats per storm-unit cell, over
   section 19's own estimate of about half a million rows and at most 5,118
   non-zero cells, requires about one nat of improvement on every non-zero cell.
   F4 is close to certain to fire whatever the truth, and its reading would be
   quoted as evidence against H-SLIDE. Condition C3.
3. **A 리 name is not a place.** Section 4.2's bounds are name counts: 1,813
   distinct address tuples against 1,427 names, 173 province-county pairs against
   the quoted figure, 263 리 names occurring in more than one place and one in
   seven. Condition C4.
4. **The 시도 column is partly a calendar clock.** Eighteen names for seventeen
   provinces; 강원도 appears only in 2021 and 2022 and 강원특별자치도 only from
   2023, 전라북도 only in 2023 and 전북특별자치도 only from 2024, and 제주시
   appears once at the wrong rung. Item A9 is a live path from the gazetteer into
   the aliasing, not `mitigated`. Condition C5.
5. **The fitted mean is a product of three things, not two**, the third being
   administrative entry multiplicity. Condition C6, written so that both outcome
   columns are reported and my having seen the clumping distribution steers
   nothing.
6. **The control identifier scheme needs a collision rule.** `canonical_fire_id`
   strips underscores and hyphens, so a colliding scheme welds distant control
   blocks silently instead of refusing, which is worse than the defect A4's fix
   repairs. Condition C7, and both traps are now pinned by tests.

## 6. The four judgements the round asked for

**The unit change and the likelihood change are both right.** Ratified in section
5 of the record, each on three arguments. The measured assignability rule is
genuinely outcome-free, and the evidence is stronger than A4 claims: M1, M2 and
M3 contain no landslide data, M4 is ordered after the scale is fixed, and the
gate thresholds are provably committed before their inputs, because the
measurements need a DEM and a boundary layer that do not exist. The gap is that
M4 has no floor and no reading rule. Condition C8.

**The support problem is fatal to the question the direction was set, and it is
not a reason to stop.** The record asks how long a burned Korean slope stays more
landslide-prone; the data reaches three years against prior art running to about
twenty-five, and every value of three is one cohort in one storm. No statistical
treatment reaches past the last observation and there is no bound to state
either. What survives is the year-zero contrast, which is a different question
and is answerable, and the outcome-free assignability and aliasing tables, which
are worth having whatever happens to the hypothesis and which gate U3 lets
terminate the direction cheaply. So: proceed, and withdraw the window.
`OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` Q10's right-censored lower bound is the one place A4's
discipline slips and condition C9 removes it, as a condition on the quantity and
not only on the sentence, because RC-003 governs sentences and a number that
exists will find a sentence.

**On reporting versus mapping, A4 is right and is right that it is worse.** The
record is a damage administration file classified by forest ownership, so item
C3's mapping mechanism is not in it, and I reached the same conclusion from the
same column before opening A4's document. A4's two reasons for rating it worse
both hold: the reporting probability and the road exposure are the same covariate
measured once, and a reporting record has no observable zero. **And C3 is not a
separate item from C2.** Post-fire administrative attention is attention to
burned land in the year or two after a named fire, which is a burned-by-calendar-
year interaction, and that interaction is precisely the term whose freedom
removes the identification. My checklist goes to v1.1 saying so.
