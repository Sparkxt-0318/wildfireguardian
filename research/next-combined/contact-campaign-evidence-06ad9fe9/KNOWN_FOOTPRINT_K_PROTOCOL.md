Each job acquires /tmp/wfg/HEAVY.lock and releases it after the child is reaped.
Each complete child, including imports/source admission/planning/recheck, has
30 seconds and 3072 MiB sampled RSS guards, plus final elapsed and self-reported

stdin is closed, its process group is owned, and cleanup is checked after exit.

pins are checked between jobs. A code change requires stopping this source
phase and reporting a new phase; active source is never edited during a run.

authenticating original raw source/masks and original request, through full
dwell and finite horizon. The second result is saved independently of the
planner claim. Full original request text and per-member history are retained;
the private thermal omission remains explicit and no history is rebound.
