# kcf-defend: where would holding the fire add the most safe departure time?

From a firefighter interview: tell the Korea Forest Service which location to hold. For each candidate (a 375 m road
cell, or a 2 km section of the known fire front), fire arrival there is delayed by 30, 60 or 120 minutes. The study
computes the population-weighted minutes of safe departure window that villages would gain in the F-3.1 deadline field,
then ranks the candidates at each issue hour of the 25 March 2025 replay (kcf-replay), across six reach curves.

**Model output on reconstructed inputs. Not what happened to people. No claim is made that any action would have
changed any outcome.**
