# Final independent figure captions

This six-figure set in `final_figures/` supersedes the four original producer plots in `analysis/results/` for final publication use. Originals and intermediate layout versions are preserved. All quantitative panels use checkpoint c651, the v3 campaign and the frozen independent score, not historical checkpoint 9c2. Exact source and export hashes are in `final_figures/FIGURE_PROVENANCE.json`.

**Figure 1. Conditional mission resolution by regional group and arm.** Each arm has four missions per group and two repetitions per mission. Stacks distinguish missions with both repetitions returning checked routes, both returning scoped centre refusals, and unresolved missions. Candidate resolution is 20/20 missions (15 route, five refusal); reference resolution is 13/20 (all route). Five groups represent four parent incidents: Uiseong–Andong and Yeongdeok share KFS_01. Refusals are not returned routes. Independent optimum/refusal evidence is scoped to the declared centre model and relaxation; runtime whole-road certificate authority is separate.

**Figure 2. Recorded request cost and jointly resolved routing latency.** Left: median complete-request wall time for all eight attempts per group/arm, including unresolved outcomes. Right: all 40 exact-input pairs versus the 26 jointly resolved route pairs, with separate denominators. All-pair medians are 8.638 s/7.281 s (reference/candidate); joint-route medians are 7.344 s/8.006 s. All 26 joint-route arrivals match. Median paired ratios (1.00015/1.07552) differ from ratios of arm medians (0.84296/1.09007). The mixture median does not establish faster successful routing. Timing ends at process completion and includes imports, cold preparation, search, runtime checks and publication. Post-exit cleanup, forecast/export, packet preparation, offline checks and reproduction are separate.

**Figure 3. Maximum recorded memory with distinct process scopes.** Each bar summarizes all eight attempts per regional group/arm. Left: the timed command's largest single-process RSS. Right: sampled aggregate process-family RSS. The 3,072 MiB guard applies to sampled-family memory; it is not a threshold on the left-hand metric. Global maxima are 2,054.547/1,492.172 MiB for largest-process RSS and 2,054.141/1,491.563 MiB for sampled-family RSS. The median paired largest-process candidate-minus-reference difference is +9.844 MiB across 40 pairs, so lower maxima do not imply universal per-pair savings. MiB denotes 2²⁰ bytes.

**Figure 4. Four disjoint probability-source categories in each hazard-source window.** The denominator is that packet's H × W cells, counted once per window. The categories are native positive q, native zero q in known-unburned cells, native zero q in known-burned cells (a structural first-new-burn zero), and missing q assigned zero under policy. They sum to 100%; assigned zero is not estimated q. Native-valid fractions include both native-zero categories. Seven windows contain no positive native q; eight have no sampled new ignition in all four members. Unknown cumulative-state counts overlap these categories and remain a separate source limitation. A null window means the whole event hazard-source grid, not necessarily the road graph domain. This is not route-occupancy support, forecast accuracy or calibrated joint-forecast evidence.

**Figure 5. Illustrative current-law routing and checking workflow.** The diagram records input binding, timed-journey search, full-journey runtime checking, preservation of all outcomes, independent checks within scope and evidence-limited reporting. It is not a measured trajectory. Directed road chords, legal waits/turns, supplied incurred exposure, destination opening and dwell, and per-member dose are retained contracts. Independent centre/contact/relaxation checks and diagnostic radiation samples do not establish an independent continuous-road radiation enclosure or global proof. Foundations are attributed to Dean (2004), Irnich and Desaulniers (2005), and Phillips and Likhachev (2011); see the reused verified APA ledger.

**Figure 6. Authentic recorded Uljin route overlay and modeled current-fire footprint.** The displayed reference/full and candidate/full repetition-1 records for Uljin 20220304T030000Z_FX1 have identical timed geometry: 512 edge legs, arrival at 3,119 s and destination dwell through 3,239 s. The source is the exact declared 375 m cell in EPSG:5179 with phase [0, 3,207.19289728573) s relative to cutoff. This single current cell has R_max equal to its native remaining time; the common full-cell phase duration T = 3,970.4642815249267 s is different. All four sampled members have no new ignition despite 80 native positive-q cells. The independently reconstructed centre dose total is about 0.867638 kJ/m², including about 0.835420 kJ/m² of supplied incurred exposure. The plot uses actual graph/trajectory coordinates and an explicitly labeled same-path overlay. It depicts a modeled source footprint, not an observed flame perimeter; it establishes neither physical safety nor distance optimality. This scored case illustration is separate from the frozen Hongseong representative fresh replay.

All packets use scalar R_max current-fire activity; 14/15 are non-exact enclosures of heterogeneous per-cell remaining times. Zero unknown-age enclosure count does not imply zero fire age. These limits apply across the figure set.
