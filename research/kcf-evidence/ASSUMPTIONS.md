# Assumptions behind the deadline field and the corridor router

One page. Each row: what is assumed, its value, the file it comes from, and what breaks if it is wrong. Paths are from the repository root; `[corridor]` means branch `codex/experimental-corridor-router-v8` (copied evidence in `research/kcf-evidence/sources/corridor-router-v8/`). "Model-relative" throughout: nothing here is field validation.

| # | Assumption | Value | Source file | What breaks if it is wrong |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Forecast step and scenario window | New-burn probability per **1 hour**; routing scenarios cover **0–3,600 s** after the input time; departures checked every 10 min | `planner/docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` §1; `docs/solve2027/DECISION_CARD.md` (leave-by section); `docs/solve2027/MODEL_CARD.md` (quote: a "375 m grid and one-hour output spacing do not establish … one-hour timing accuracy") | Ignition times inside the hour are drawn uniformly, so minute-level flame timing is not information. Every leave-by is capped by the horizon (≤ 3,480 s), so issue-by times are degenerate at 1 h (`research/harness-2026-11/F-3.1/RESULTS.md` §6). A fire that moves faster or slower than the hourly rule moves every deadline. |
| 2 | Cell size and cell-as-flame | **375 m** cells (EPSG:5179); a whole cell is treated as flaming and uniformly emitting once ignited | `data/routing/graphs/*/graph.json` (`grid.resolution` = 375.0; fetched by `tools/fetch_routing_inputs.py`); `integration/src/wfg_latest/bridge.py` (`spatial_interpretation`) | A road crossing a corner of a cell is blocked as if the whole cell burned (conservative for contact), while a real fire front inside a cell is not resolved. Router and closed-footprint rules differ on edge/corner touches (`F-3.1/RESULTS.md` §2, "cert" vs "router"). |
| 3 | Road-graph size per region (OSM snapshot, 2022–2025) | Hongseong 915 nodes / 1,806 edges; Miryang 1,494 / 3,115; Uljin 9,372 / 18,909; Uiseong–Andong 23,158 / 43,826; Yeongdeok 56,369 / 111,924. The 3 Caldor cases of the 273 use a graph that is not in the fetched inputs (size not reported here) | `data/routing/graphs/<region>/graph.json` (counts of `nodes`, `edges`; SHA-256 in `data/routing/INPUT_FILES.json`); Caldor count from `sources/corridor-router-v8/receipts.csv` (`incident`) | Cost scales with edges: the six planner caches use the two largest graphs, so timings do not transfer to small graphs, nor to unlisted regions. Roads missing from OSM, or private/no-access ways excluded, are absent or wrongly absent from every route and no-route proof. |
| 4 | Driving speed | Every edge **assumed 40 km/h** on the chord between mapped nodes, rounded **up** to whole-second ticks; missing one-way assumed two-way; "no road/passability or wait-location field validation". Implied chord speed ≤ 40.0 km/h (Hongseong median 35.2 km/h, computed from `travel_ticks` and node coordinates) | `provenance.interpretation` in each `graph.json`; `docs/solve2027/DECISION_CARD.md` ("Outside the decision's scope") | Real speeds on gravel, mountain or smoke-covered roads are lower: arrivals later, deadlines earlier, "checked" routes may fail. Rounding up makes short edges slower than 40 km/h (conservative); straight chords shorten curvy roads (optimistic). |
| 5 | Four scenarios, cells drawn independently | **4 members** per check; besides fire already burning, each member's new ignitions are independent per-cell draws from the marginal probabilities, at uniform times in the hour. "Marginal probabilities do not define a joint law" | `docs/solve2027/MODEL_CARD.md` and `DECISION_CARD.md` (quote of `draw_ignitions`); `integration/src/wfg_latest/bridge.py` docstring | Four draws are a thin sample of futures; real spread is spatially correlated, so a connected burn corridor across a road is under- or over-represented. In 7 of 15 evaluated windows no native positive future probability exists; in 8 no member has any sampled ignition (`research/routing/INDEPENDENT_REPORT.v1.md`), so the hazard there is only the fire already burning. |
| 6 | Burn duration | An ignited cell burns for **T ≈ 3,970.46 s** (point residence 11.52 s + front crossing 375 m at 341 m/h), emitting 4,686.6 W/m² at radiative fraction 0.30 | `integration/src/wfg_latest/bridge.py` docstring (latest documented default) | T exceeds the 3,600 s window (arithmetic from the two values), so within the hour an ignited cell never burns out and its roads never reopen. If real burn-out is faster, roads reopen and leave-by times are later. (Separate 5 h / 8 h packets: allowing re-entry vs not changes 14,954 of 175,392 verdict units, `F-3.1/RESULTS.md`, multi-hour section.) |
| 7 | Heat limit never binds | Max flux over all cells/bins/members **0.702 kW/m²** against a **10 kW/m²** peak limit (14× margin); max non-flame flux 0.01555 kW/m² ⇒ dose ≤ 56 kJ/m² against 100; so "full policy = flame contact" on all six caches | `research/harness-2026-11/F-3.1/RESULTS.md` §3; `F-3.1/DESIGN.md` §2 (recomputed per packet at run time) | The radiation model is not calibrated to measured wildfire flux (Physics HOLD, `README.md`). If true flux is ~14× higher, peak binds and the contact-only field is no longer exact: it becomes a relaxation and every witness must pass the full checker. |
| 8 | Destinations | Candidate destinations are OpenStreetMap-tagged shelters, assembly points, schools, community centres and 회관 halls: **not an official shelter designation**; arrival requires the stay (2-min dwell in the cache requests) inside the opening window | `docs/solve2027/DECISION_CARD.md`; `planner/docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` §4.2; `planner/cache/*/*.json` (`dwell_s`, `destination_window`) | A routed-to place may be closed, unsafe, full or not a shelter. Deadlines are to "a model-checked place", not to an assigned shelter. Destination suitability is explicitly outside the check. |
| 9 | No congestion or road state | Free-flow vehicle from the road entry point; no walking, boarding, congestion, closures or smoke; waiting only at nodes the graph marks `waitable` | `planner/docs/LASTMILE.md` ("Results are model results for vehicles from the road entry point: no walking, boarding time, congestion, road closures or smoke"); `graph.json` provenance | Evacuation traffic, blocked or contraflow roads and smoke slow or stop journeys; real leave-by times are earlier. Many vehicles on one road is outside the single-vehicle check. |
| 10 | Forecast skill | The routing forecast (c651) is not shown to beat simple baselines. On the six-US-fire, 273-window held-out set the later selected model `d4_wind` has Brier 0.0049269 vs persistence 0.0049968; paired 95% interval of the difference [−1.38e-4, +2.29e-5] **includes zero**; criterion "false". In the same file `c651_mc` has Brier 0.0098522 (difference vs persistence +4.86e-3, interval [+2.66e-3, +8.57e-3], i.e. **worse**). On the Korean 2025 secondary reconstruction `c651_mc` is better (BSS interval [0.220, 0.352]) | `research/next-combined/canonical-summary-691cc162/RESULTS.json` (`heldout.paired`, `heldout.models`, `korean2025_secondary_reconstruction`, `retrospective_superiority_gate`); `docs/solve2027/MODEL_CARD.md` | Hazard cells may be missing or invented relative to what burns. A route "checked" against a skill-less forecast is only checked against that forecast. The deadline field is exact for the given hazard; it adds nothing to forecast accuracy. The held-out `c651_*` rows are as scored in that file; I did not check that its rollout setup matches the routing configuration. |

**Not covered above:** the corridor router's missing-input policy (zero-fill vs native coverage required) is Figure B and `FIGURE_NUMBERS.json`; 163 of its 189 permissive routes touched a cell-hour with no native forecast value (`sources/corridor-router-v8/receipts.csv`, column `unsupported_cell_hours`).

**Evaluated host.** The recorded per-check compute comes from the host recorded in `planner/cache/INDEX.json` (macOS-27.0.1 arm64, 15 CPUs, Python 3.12.1). Timing elsewhere differs.
