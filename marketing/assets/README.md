# Marketing assets — provenance

All assets in this directory are **AI-generated** (created 2026-08-07 via the
Artlist generation service) and are marketing material only. None of them are
photographs of real people or real events, and none may be presented as such.
Faces are synthetic; any resemblance to real persons is coincidental.

| file | generator | purpose |
|---|---|---|
| `app_icon_concept.png` | Nano Banana 2 (1:1, 2K, downscaled to 1024) | app-icon design concept: shield, flame, escape path to a safe house. Final store icons should be redrawn as vector from this concept. |
| `hero_banner.jpg` | Nano Banana 2 (16:9, 2K) | website/store hero. Copy space on the right third. |
| `ad_creative_family.jpg` | Nano Banana 2 (4:5, 2K) | social ad targeting adult children ("watch over your parents"). Headline space top quarter. |
| `ad_creative_elder.jpg` | Nano Banana 2 (4:5, 2K) | poster/print creative for senior-facing channels. Text space bottom quarter. |
| `promo_video.mp4` | Kling 2.1 (image-to-video from the hero, 5 s, 720p) | short promo clip for social/YouTube pre-roll bumper. |

Usage rules (see `docs/app/AD_CAMPAIGN.md` §compliance):

- Never pair these images with copy implying a real rescue, a real family, or
  a government endorsement.
- If a platform or jurisdiction requires AI-generation disclosure in ads,
  disclose it.
- The imagery is deliberately **hopeful** (no burning homes, no people in
  danger). Keep it that way in any derivative — fear-exploiting disaster
  imagery violates the campaign's brand rules and most ad-platform policies.
